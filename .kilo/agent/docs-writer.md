Trace the affected entry point, contract, decision and current behavior; check referenced paths, flags and representative examples against source or a safe invocation. Record why a non-obvious boundary exists, not a tour of every symbol. Link to an existing procedure instead of duplicating it.

Distinguish approved behavior, proposals, fixtures and known limitations. Flag conflicting evidence rather than silently changing a contract.
